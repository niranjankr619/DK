How a milestone gets paid

DezinersKnot connects clients to designers and holds the money in between. Nothing is released until work is approved, and we take a cut from both sides of every milestone.

12%

of every milestone, charged to both sides — 5% added on top for the client, 7% deducted from the designer's payout.

Almost all of it reaches the bottom line. The gateway fee is passed through to the client, and the only cost we carry is a flat payout fee of ₹6 to ₹15 per transfer — the same whether the milestone is ₹2,000 or ₹2,00,000.

1

Award and contract

A brief goes public, proposals come in, one designer is chosen. Issuing a contract closes the board, and the designer holds the next move.

Two asymmetries, both deliberate

Edits travel one way. When a designer asks for changes the client accepts or declines them, with no counter-offer. And once a designer has accepted, the client can no longer decline — the only remaining move is to activate and pay.

2

When a contract ends before it starts

A decline does not kill the opportunity. The client either re-issues to someone who already proposed, or reopens the brief to the public.

3

Two activations, not one

Accepting locks the contract. The client then activates it, and separately activates and pays for milestone one — that second payment is what actually starts the work. Every later milestone repeats the same step.

4

Running the milestones

Every milestone is funded before work starts and released only after sign-off. The revision loop is capped at three rounds.

5

Where the money and the checks flow

Money in through the Cashfree gateway, money out through Payouts, identity checks through Secure ID. Both wallets are funded from a whitelisted DezinersKnot account.

8

Ending a contract

Exits sit at both ends of a contract. The window in between, once a milestone is funded but not yet approved, is closed by design.

Exit open

Before activation

No milestone has been funded, so no money has moved. Either side walks away and nothing is owed.

No exit

Funded and in progress

A milestone paid for but not yet approved cannot be exited. Both the money and the contract stay where they are until the client signs off.

Closes itself

After completion

The final milestone is approved and paid. The contract closes on its own with no action from either side.
